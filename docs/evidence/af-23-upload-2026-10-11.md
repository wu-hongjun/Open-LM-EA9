## Installation and reconnect sequence

Only after exactly one qualified, fresh, unblocked cap connection appears:

```sh
dist/ea9ctl inventory
dist/ea9ctl apply-af contax-zeiss-50-f1-4 --idle
# Owner physically unplugs and reconnects the same adapter through its cap.
dist/ea9ctl inventory
dist/ea9ctl verify --idle
```

The native uploader takes a fresh two-pass complete flash/auxiliary backup,
qualifies the loader and MCU, synchronizes recovery files, performs its final
idle preflight read and durably journals the transaction before START.
Planned upload is one START and 320 acknowledged 64-byte pages. No extra report,
read, retry, padding or end marker is allowed after upload and before physical
reconnect. The adapter stays off the camera until verification succeeds.

Verification must use the same unit on a new OS connection and must compare
the full application/profile, known metadata, protected loader/gap, remaining
flash and factory auxiliary data. The loader's six adjacent-RAM metadata bytes
are excluded according to the previously documented protocol boundary. Native
verification is followed by an independent offline comparison of the retained
before/after backups and journal. Actual results will be appended, not inferred
from acknowledgement success.

Private frozen products, prior state, exact commands/logs and script hashes are
retained under the ignored `references/private/af-23-upload-2026-10-11/` directory.
The [public JSON](af-23-upload-2026-10-11.json) retains stage, hashes and results
without unit identifiers or backup payloads.
