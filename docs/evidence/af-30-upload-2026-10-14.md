photo was saved; battery removal was required**. The adapter was still on the
camera. Further AF 3.0 trials are held. The owner was told to stop, keep the
camera unpowered, and not restart or retry this image. No stock restoration or
new hardware operation was performed after the report.

Actual MF/AF-S, Pre-AF, exposure, physical iris/focus-ring and aperture display

Reported focusing does not establish saved sharpness, exposure or lifecycle.

phase-driven interruption/raw-echo boundary missing from AF 3.0 for five tested

idle, one MF photo if safe and shutdown. No repeat of AF 3.0 is requested to
