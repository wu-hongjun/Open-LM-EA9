## Reconnect and camera gates

For any future upload the loader remains armed. Physically unplug and
reconnect the same adapter through its cap, keep the updater closed and adapter
off the camera, then run normal native `verify --idle`. Require complete image,
profile, known metadata, boot/protected gap, retained upper flash and factory
auxiliary equality against the fresh backup. No camera use before readback.

For a future replacement after strict verification, follow
[the A7C II runbook](../af-s-test.md): first a
loaded-lens MF lifecycle/display/saved-exposure check with physical f/1.4 and
focus infinity, still-photo A, EV0/Auto ISO and Pre-AF Off where available. Only
when those observations are normal proceed to one bounded AF-S attempt.
Unknown settings remain unverified. A reported failure is sufficient; an old
unavailable photo is not required.

The aperture is a fixed nominal declaration; actual iris remains Unknown and
only the lens ring controls stop-down. Completion/Stop is not proof of physical
rest or optical lock. Camera acceptance of measured coast, conservative unknown
motion and equal factory positions is unqualified. Stop for grinding, repeated
stop impacts or continuous abnormal drive; switch off and remove the battery if
movement continues, then do not retry.
